# WAM Instructor Lab v2.0 — No-Notes Certification

Production browser/PWA course for learning to teach the Working Actor Masterclass curriculum.

## Training standard

The instructional core remains 360 planned active minutes. Completion alone does **not** unlock WAM Ready certification. The learner must also complete the No-Notes Mastery Lab:

- reconstruct every authored workshop sequence from memory;
- score at least 90% on each workshop precision check;
- record a 90-second no-notes workshop map;
- record a 45-second randomly assigned exercise-direction rep;
- review both recordings against the instructor rubrics;
- clear all 12 workshop gates in one clean gauntlet without opening the lesson-plan library;
- finish the six-hour course; and
- score at least 90% on the final mastery exam.

The 12 certification maps are Voice & Movement Thu/Fri/Sat/Sun, Pressure in Action I/II/III, Listening & Responding I/II, Acting for the Camera I/II, and Rehearse Like a Pro. Guest workshops remain operational/integration training because the guest instructors own their workshop content.

## Detail layer

Each workshop includes its purpose, memory spine, complete timed sequence, exercise directions, facilitator prompts, likely teaching traps, recovery moves, read-aloud coaching, and a retrieval/certification mode.

## Spoken coach

The browser voice engine prefers higher-quality English voices whose system names indicate Natural/Neural/Online/Enhanced/Premium when available. Speech is chunked into shorter thought units with light pacing variation. The exact acoustic quality depends on the voices exposed by the operating system/browser.

## Privacy and storage

Progress is stored in `localStorage` when available. Audio/video recordings are stored locally in IndexedDB and are not uploaded by the app. Progress can be exported/imported as JSON.

## Run

For the full microphone/camera/PWA feature set, host this directory over HTTPS. The included `netlify.toml` supports a static Netlify deployment. `WAM_Instructor_Lab_v2_Standalone.html` is supplied separately for quick local viewing, though browser security rules can limit microphone/camera and persistent storage when a file is opened directly.
